# Khmer Lunar Calendar (Chhankitek / ចន្ទគតិ) & Era System

### Cultural & Traditional Cambodian Calendar Framework

In Cambodia, two concurrent calendar systems govern civil, religious, and cultural life:
1. **Suriyakitek (សុរិយគតិ / Solar Buddhist Era):** Civil calendar based on solar Gregorian days, where the civil Buddhist Era is calculated as $\text{BE} = \text{CE} + 543$.
2. **Chhankitek (ចន្ទគតិ / Traditional Lunar Calendar):** Astronomical lunar calendar governing Buddhist holy days (*Thngai Sil* / ថ្ងៃសីល), Khmer New Year (*Moha Songkran* / មហាសង្ក្រាន្ត), Pchum Ben (*ភ្ជុំបិណ្ឌ*), and Water Festival (*ព្រះរាជពិធីបុណ្យអុំទូក*).

## 3. Formal Civil Invocation Syntax

In official royal decrees (*Preah Reach Kram*), ministry declarations (*Prakas*), and traditional legal contracts, the opening dateline follows this formal sequence:

$$\text{ថ្ងៃ... [Phase] ខែ... ឆ្នាំ [Zodiac] [Sak] ព.ស. [BE]}$$

### Examples
- **Year 2024:** `ព.ស. ២៥៦៨ ឆ្នាំរោង ឆស័ក`
- **Year 2025:** `ព.ស. ២៥៦៩ ឆ្នាំម្សាញ់ សប្តស័ក`
- **Year 2026:** `ព.ស. ២៥៧០ ឆ្នាំមមី អដ្ឋស័ក`

## 4. Lunar Months (ខែចន្ទគតិ)

The lunar year contains 12 months (or 13 during an intercalary leap year *Adhikameas* / អធិកមាស):

1. **មិគសិរ** (Mikhase / Margashirsha) — 29 days
2. **បុស្ស** (Boh / Pushya) — 30 days
3. **មាឃ** (Meak / Magha) — 29 days
4. **ផល្គុន** (Phalkun / Phalguna) — 30 days
5. **ចេត្រ** (Chetr / Chaitra) — 29 days (Khmer New Year occurs here)
6. **ពិសាខ** (Pisak / Vaishakha) — 30 days (Visak Bochea holy day)
7. **ជេស្ឋ** (Ches / Jyeshtha) — 29 days (or 30 in leap-day years *Chhnam Chhantrea-titea*)
8. **អាសាឍ** (Asath / Ashadha) — 30 days (Vassa rainy retreat begins)
   - *(During leap months, doubled into បឋមាសាឍ First Asath & ទុតិយាសាឍ Second Asath)*
9. **ស្រាពណ៍** (Srap / Shravana) — 29 days
10. **ភទ្របទ** (Phatrabot / Bhadrapada) — 30 days (Pchum Ben festival)
11. **អស្សុជ** (Assoch / Ashvina) — 29 days (End of Buddhist Lent)
12. **កត្តិក** (Kadek / Karttika) — 30 days (Water Festival / Bon Om Touk)
